How quickly can you implement taxi dispatch services for my business? +

We can typically implement our taxi call answering service within 2-3 weeks from contract signing.

The implementation process includes understanding your business requirements, configuring systems, training our team on your specific protocols, and testing the integration.

For more straightforward setups, we can expedite the process to as little as one week.

Will your taxi dispatch center work with my existing booking software? +

Yes, HAULXIFY works with all major taxi dispatch software systems including Autocab, iCabbi, Taxi Butler, Cabmaster, TaxiCaller, and many others.

Our technical team handles the integration process, ensuring smooth data flow between our call center and your dispatch system.

If you use custom or proprietary software, we’ll work with your IT team to establish appropriate integration points.

How do you ensure the security of my customer information? +

HAULXIFY maintains strict data security protocols that comply with international standards.

All our systems incorporate encryption, secure access controls, and regular security audits.

Our staff undergoes thorough background checks and receives training on data protection and privacy regulations.

We implement comprehensive data handling policies to protect sensitive customer information and comply with relevant regulations.

What hours does your taxi call answering service operate? +

Our taxi dispatch call center operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year.

This ensures your customers can book taxis at any time, including nights, weekends, and holidays.

Our round-the-clock service is particularly valuable for airport transfers, late-night transportation, and emergency travel needs when in-house staff would typically be unavailable.

How are your taxi dispatch services priced? +

Our pricing is transparent and based on an hourly rate for our full-time or dedicated employees.

This structure allows us to accommodate various business sizes and needs efficiently.

With our dedicated agents, you benefit from consistent service quality without the overhead of managing in-house staff.

Contact us for a personalized quote tailored to your specific business requirements and anticipated call volume.

We are here to help you optimize your operations and enhance customer satisfaction.

What exactly does a taxi call answering service do? +

A taxi call answering service manages all incoming calls for your taxi business.

This includes dispatch services, scheduling rides, providing fare estimates, handling customer inquiries, and ensuring seamless communication between drivers and customers.

How does a taxi answering service benefit my small business? +

A taxi answering service can provide numerous benefits to both large and small businesses.

It ensures that your taxi company is always available to answer the calls, after-hours included.

Furthermore, professional call center representatives fluent in English can provide excellent customer service and avoid potential losses from poor customer service.

What if I need help after normal business hours? +

Our taxi answering services are available 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.
