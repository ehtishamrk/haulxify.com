FAQ

Common Questions

How does the $4/hour pricing work?

+

You're billed for the actual hours our team works on your account. This includes load finding, negotiations, paperwork, and coordination. We track hours transparently and provide detailed reports.

What does "Free Weekends" mean?

+

If you haul loads on Saturday or Sunday, we don't charge for dispatch services on those days. It's our way of supporting truckers who work weekends.

How do the billing discounts work?

+

Monthly billing saves you 15% off the hourly rate ($3.40/hour). Quarterly billing saves you 25% ($3.00/hour). The longer the commitment, the more you save.

Is there a minimum commitment?

+

No long-term contracts required for weekly billing. Monthly and quarterly plans require the respective minimum periods but can be canceled at any time after.

What payment methods do you accept?

+

We accept all major credit cards, ACH transfers, Zelle, and PayPal. Invoices are sent weekly/monthly/quarterly based on your billing cycle.
